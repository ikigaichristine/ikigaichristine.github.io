Ikigai

Not the diagram.
The reason you get up,
which is usually smaller than that
and already in the room.

Bullpen

Tuesday. Nobody watching.
We are not fixing the arm.
We are fixing what she tells herself
between the bad inning and the next pitch.

Six a.m.

The lot is empty because
the good part is early.
Three hours in, it is the view
from the postcard, and nothing like it.

Sand

Cool until ten.
Two touches, a call,
a dive that was probably unnecessary.
Nothing else gets in.

Mat

Some weeks it holds.
Some weeks it is four minutes
in a hotel room in a city
I could not draw from memory.
Both count.

Bench

Wire, solder, a stone I paid too much for.
Most of it goes to other people.
The good ones close
with a sound you feel in your thumb.

Table

Open the second bottle.
Say the true thing about it
instead of the impressive thing.
Let it run past eleven.

Dressed

Not vanity. Notice.
The day you intend to have,
worn where people can see it.

Leave the people around you better off.
Be specific about how.

Christine Page
